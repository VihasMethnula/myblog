Having a browser (a good one) is a must to continue human existence, So in throughout my life I have used number of browsers, But at the end of the day, one shines among them (disclaimer: Totally depend on your own preference and here I am showing my preference)

## Zen Browser: Calmer internet

![Pasted image 20261123125443.png](/images/Pasted%20image%2020261123125443.png)

Now this is a really good browser not only it lets you surf internet safer but also it is so lightweight.
The interface is gorgeous Sometimes when I browse for long time i forget that I am in a browser its just like I am inside internet.

Vertical Tabs keep me focused and productive on my work ![Pasted image 20261123125743.png](/images/Pasted%20image%2020261123125743.png)

Honestly as a long time user of Brave, I feel like i should jump permanently to this. However for cutting edge privacy I still would choose Brave: especially its Tor facility. At the end of the day I just feel Zen is next level. BTW its also updating regularly it's Developers are cooking it.
