I added more services to my homelab, Nextcloud and Vaultwarden. Its altogether 4 services in my homelab. 

Nextcloud is basically the Google Drive but local, free, better. For storage I currently use a 32GB sd card but its going to evolve in the near future.

Also nextcloud is coming with lots of things like calender, teams, mails. I disabled them all as I don't want them. I did that to lower RAM usage. I also use a tool called nextcloud-redis. Its a docker container and used for caching purpose. It make the nextcloud much smoother and efficient.

![Pasted image 20261003193257.png](/images/Pasted%20image%2020261003193257.png)

Speaking of vaultwarden its my password manager. Its really good free, opensource. Its bitwarden compatible so I just install bitwarden browser extention and vola!

![Pasted image 20261003193009.png](/images/Pasted%20image%2020261003193009.png)

RAM usages are also not bad at all. Impressive. I think its mainly because I disabled some extras in nextcloud. Otherwise its going to be like 1.5GB. Anyway I honestly think its really cool. Like imagine maintaining your own Google drive and Password manager.
