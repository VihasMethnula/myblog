Jellyfin and Pi-hole, both are really amazing tools. I use those everyday.

Pi-hole act as a ad blocker for my laptop and it really good, but for me its little unuseful as I already have brave ad blocker. But i still use pi-hole because brave cant block all and also when I am using internet for somethig else than brave, Pi-hole covers them all.

Jellyfin on the other hand its really handy when it comes to watching movies. Its free and open source. 

![Pasted image 20261003112608.png](/images/Pasted%20image%2020261003112608.png)

Also jellyfin comes with music listening facility as well So, its like a personal free alternative to both Spotify and Netflix.

At the same time the RAM usages are not bad, Its really efficient. BTW I don't use transcoding So, its using much lower resources
