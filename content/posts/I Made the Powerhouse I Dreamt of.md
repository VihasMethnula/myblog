### What it consist of

- **A 4S battery pack**, which is the heart of the whole thing.
- **A BMS** with under-voltage and short-circuit protection. This is the part that keeps a mistake from turning into a fire.
- **An ESP32** as the brain.
- **An INA226** to measure voltage and current properly
- **An OLED** so I can see what's going on
- **A MOSFET load switch**, so the ESP32 decides when power goes out.


Now this might not be a cutting edge perfect power bank that you can gurantee 100% safe to use. But its definetly a fun project and pretty much usable in my daily life with my supervision. So, At the end of the I am happy of what I've made.
