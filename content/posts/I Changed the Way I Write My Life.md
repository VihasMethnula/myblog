I've decided to transition my journaling to the Bullet Journaling (BuJo) method. This method allows me to write journals effectively more organized with my thoughts.

This system was inspired by Ryder Carrol (Author of Bullet Journaling Method). And honestly I think its cool

This system follows a clean structure and a noting system that is far beyond the ordinary journaling method I used

My Journal consist of,
 - A Monthly log along with monthly goals
 - A day log 
 - Few custom trackers I designed to improve things I really need to do 

Whats beautiful about this system is that its noting system, In short Its called as NAME.

N - Notes
A - Actions
M - Mood
E - Events 

Everyday I follow this method to reflect, refine my life, Bullet Journaling just makes it 10 times easier.

#### Why this change?

The previous way I was writing my life felt too unstructured. Bullet journaling provides the perfect balance as a structured journal.

At the end of the day I honestly think its a nice tweak to my life I made.
